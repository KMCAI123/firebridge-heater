## 2. Mandatory safe-use conditions

- **Supervise every use.** Remain physically present and awake. Do not run a heater using this project while sleeping, away from the installation, or unable to monitor it. Do not leave children, vulnerable people or animals dependent on it without a capable supervising adult.
- **Check actual operation.** Before each start, verify the installation is safe and the correct heater is selected. Observe ignition, running, shutdown and completion of the manufacturer's cooldown sequence at the heater. Verify the manufacturer's controller and physical indicators; do not rely solely on FireBridge or Apple Home.
- **Do not enable unattended automation.** Do not create schedules, geofencing, remote starts, voice routines, scenes or restart rules that could operate a heater without an adult physically present. Prevent other household members, shared accounts or integrations from starting it unexpectedly.
- **Follow the exact manufacturer instructions and applicable rules.** Use only a heater intended for the location and application, with the specified installation, fuel, electrical supply, protection, ventilation, combustion-air intake, exhaust routing and clearances. Obtain qualified installation or inspection where required. This project does not make an unsuitable heater or installation safe.
- **Maintain independent protection.** Use appropriate, working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms installed and maintained according to their instructions and local requirements. Arrange professional maintenance as applicable. Alarms supplement supervision; neither they nor this software eliminate combustion hazards.
- **Preserve all original safeguards.** Never defeat overheat protection, sensors, fuses, fault lockouts, exhaust requirements or manufacturer interlocks. Keep the original controller available. Do not repeatedly restart a faulted heater or issue repeated commands when acknowledgment is missing.
- **Preserve normal cooldown.** Do not use a smart plug, unplugging, loss of Fire TV power or removal of this app as a normal heater-off method. Maintain heater power for the manufacturer's shutdown/cooldown process. In an emergency, follow the manufacturer's emergency instructions; do not endanger yourself to preserve cooldown.
- **Stop using the integration if behavior is abnormal or uncertain.** Use the original controller and manufacturer procedures when safe. If there is smoke, fire, a carbon monoxide alarm, suspected fumes or danger to people, leave the hazardous area and contact emergency services from safety. Do not wait for software confirmation or attempt troubleshooting in a dangerous area.
- **Secure access.** Protect pairing codes, setup URLs and accounts; use a trusted network and do not expose setup interfaces to the internet. Review who can operate accessories and remove unsafe automations. Recheck operation after any software, firmware, network or hardware change.

These conditions supplement the manufacturer's requirements; they never authorize a use the manufacturer prohibits. If requirements conflict, do not operate through this project until the conflict is resolved by a qualified professional.
